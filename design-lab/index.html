Four real product systems,
applied to the same dashboard.

Each direction below pulls its palette, type scale, radii and button anatomy from the actual design system of a shipped product — not from my taste. The data and the structure are identical across all four.

Paper

ElevenLabs design system — warm cream ground, stone hairlines, 20px card radii, pill buttons. Ink on cream, one yellow note.

ElevenLabs · warm editorial

Studio

Apple design system — near-white canvas, one vivid blue, type as the voice with negative tracking that tightens at scale. The product IS the design.

Apple · near-white restraint

Atelier

Duolingo design system — bright rounded cards, Feather-bold Nunito, one green at 48–64px. Playful without being childish.

Duolingo · bright and rounded

Nocturne

Authkit design system — frosted-glass cathedral at midnight, one violet CTA, blueprint blue reduced to decorative strokes. Glass done tastefully.

Authkit · frosted glass
